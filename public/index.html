One door into European sovereign debt.

Puerta is a platform in development that will connect regulated asset managers with professional investors through tokenized units of government-bond funds.

Contact us

For asset managers, custodians and institutional partners. We are building the first version and looking for asset manager partners.

Who does what

Asset managers
Run the fund. The manager stays the regulated party and keeps full responsibility for the portfolio, pricing and fund documentation.
Professional investors
Subscribe to and redeem fund units at net asset value, with each unit recorded as a token. Investor onboarding and order handling are carried out by appropriately licensed entities.
Puerta Labs
Builds the technology that connects the two: token logic, the investor interface, reporting, and integration with fund registers and custodians.

Where we start

We are starting with Spanish government debt and designing the format so it extends to other European sovereign issuers.

Units follow a fund's rolling portfolio of government bonds. Access is limited to professional investors.
